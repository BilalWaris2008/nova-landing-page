Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and how it is used.

Who we are

NOVA is a website project made by Muhammad Bilal Waris. In this policy, "we" means the owner of this site. You can reach us at hello@nova.example.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you type it into the signup form. We use it to contact you about NOVA.
  • Anonymous visit data. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see the approximate country, device type, browser and operating system of visitors. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
  • Technical data. Like any website, when your browser loads this page, the servers that deliver it (such as Vercel, Google Fonts and content delivery networks like jsDelivr, cdnjs and jQuery) can see your IP address and browser details.

How we use it

We use your email to reply to your signup and to tell you about NOVA. We use visit data to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives the email you submit and forwards it to us.
  • Vercel hosts this site and provides the analytics.
  • Google Fonts and CDNs deliver fonts and code libraries.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies itself, and Vercel Web Analytics works without cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Third-party services may follow their own practices, so please see their policies.

How long we keep it

We keep signup emails until you ask us to delete them or we no longer need them. Formspree may also keep submissions in its own archive for a limited time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the email you gave us, or to stop emailing you, at any time. Write to hello@nova.example.

How we keep data safe

This site is served over HTTPS and the signup form sends your email over an encrypted connection. We only ask for an email address. We do not collect passwords or payment details on this site.

Children

This site is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
